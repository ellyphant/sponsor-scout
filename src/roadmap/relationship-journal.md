A sent proposal is the beginning of a relationship, not its outcome. Give Elly a private place to record what happened in her inbox and what she promised to do next.

## What it looks like

- Add a dated note or reply excerpt to a sponsor and the specific events it concerns.
- Record clear milestones such as Interested, Not now, Introduction offered, Introduction made and Declined.
- Before another proposal, see prior conversations, open commitments and relevant context beside the new case.
- Correct a mistaken outcome without erasing the original record of who entered it and when.

## Key details

- The approved initial-build scope includes drafts, decisions and delivery records. This future item adds operator-recorded relationship context after outreach; it does not add an inbox connection.
- Conversation outcomes remain separate from qualification, review and delivery states. Accepted or Delivered does not mean Interested.
- Interest is not a commitment to fund an event. Record any claimed commitment with its actual terms and supporting context, never an invented amount.
- Not now does not silently become permanent Do not contact. Explicit opt-outs must continue to block outreach and cannot be cleared by an optimistic note.
- Notes remain private to the operator and do not change CoHost Club or rewrite earlier drafts.

Preserve sponsor/event identity and the original outreach record when attaching outcomes. Relationship milestones are explicit operator assertions with provenance, not automatic model training or inferred conversion events.
