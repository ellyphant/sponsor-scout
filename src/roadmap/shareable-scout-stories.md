Give community builders a concrete reason to try their own sponsorship desk. A deliberately published case shows how evidence becomes a relevant introduction, while private work stays private.

## What it looks like

- Choose a fictional sample case or a real case explicitly cleared for sharing.
- Preview a read-only story containing approved evidence, event-fit reasoning and the human decision boundary.
- Review redactions before creating a share link, then revoke future access when needed.
- Link interested builders to the source and setup guidance only once Elly has authorized public release.

## Key details

- Sample scenarios, setup documentation and licensing guidance are in the approved initial-build scope. This future item adds a publication workflow beyond the private console, not the basic demo or open-source deliverables.
- Fictional examples are the default. Sample, Recorded and Live research dates remain unambiguous; a shared replay cannot pretend a new run is happening.
- Real cases require permission to share their contents. Remove contacts, addresses, private notes, unapproved letters and sensitive source material by default, then review every included field.
- Sharing an operator's case does not confer permission to republish a host's event data. Revocation cannot retrieve screenshots or downloaded copies.
- No public signup funnel, public prospect directory or automatic sponsor outreach. Publishing a story does not publish the console or repository.

~~~
Publish a deliberately cleared artifact rather than exposing a private run through a public link. Keep sample isolation and the default-deny access boundary; no live service calls are needed to view a recorded story.
~~~
