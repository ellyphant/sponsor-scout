Learn what made a useful introduction, not merely which email was sent. At the end of a season, Scout connects recorded outcomes with the evidence and decisions that preceded them.

## What it looks like

- Review plans alongside explicit interest, declined opportunities, introductions and documented commitments.
- Astra identifies recurring fit, timing and contact problems, linking every observation to the cases behind it.
- Separate promising patterns from questions that need more evidence.
- Elly can approve a named, reversible set of research preferences for the next season, or leave the existing approach unchanged.

## Key details

- Requires Season Partner Plans and the Relationship Journal. Outcomes come from attributed operator records or participant confirmations, never inferred email opens or successful delivery.
- Show sample sizes, missing responses and unresolved outcomes. Small, selectively contacted samples do not establish causal lift or predictive sponsorship probabilities.
- Keep the original four-factor qualification rubric and historical cases intact. Preference changes must be visible, scoped and reversible, not silent score rewriting.
- Saved rejection reasons are feedback, not automatic model training. Astra proposes lessons; Elly chooses what changes.
- Report commitments only with their actual supporting terms. Do not invent revenue, return on investment or attribution.
- Learning remains private to the desk; no cross-operator data pooling is implied.
