02 · The problem

The hard part is not
finding company names.

It is knowing which company has a credible reason to support this audience, right now, and reaching the right person without inventing the case.

Host needs live in event listings. Sponsor signals live across announcements, event pages, and team profiles. A trustworthy introduction depends on bringing them together.

✕A well-funded company is not automatically a sponsor for this event.
03 · Real demand

Start with real demand.

Every run begins with upcoming CoHost Club events explicitly seeking sponsorship. Each opportunity keeps its host, audience, date, and exact ask attached.

Founder dinners Investor salons Operator meetups Conferences Hackathons

The hackathon is where Scout was built, not a limit on the events it serves. CoHost Club remains separate. Scout connects only through its read-only HTTP API. Hosts keep their home in CoHost Club.

05 · The match

One sponsor.
A few real reasons to connect.

≤3genuinely relevant events, chosen by city or region, audience, and vertical. Each match preserves the host's own ask.
1warm email that explains the fit and asks whether the recipient would like an introduction.
✕No guessed addresses.  No invented budgets.  No padded matches.

A missing public email does not waste the research. The draft waits for a recipient Elly supplies and approves.

06 · The boundary

Research can be autonomous.
Outreach cannot.

Elly reads the sources, checks the contact, and edits the letter. Final confirmation names the exact recipient, message, selected events, managed sender, Reply-To, and delivery mode.

◇Change the message or sending settings and approval expires.
◇Changed event details, or an inability to check current eligibility, stops dispatch for a new review.
■Astra cannot approve, send, or change CoHost Club. Sponsor outreach requires Elly's explicit approval of that exact email.

09 · Open source, private by default

Open source.
Private by default.

Open

Sponsor Scout is built from scratch so community builders can inspect how evidence becomes a proposal, run their own private desk, and improve the workflow. MIT is the intended license, subject to confirmation.

Private

Contacts, drafts, and run history stay private by default. Shared examples must be fictional or explicitly cleared and redacted. CoHost Club stays outside this repository, with no shared code, database, or sign-in. Public release requires Elly's permission.
