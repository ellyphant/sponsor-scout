Scout becomes useful between deliberate research runs. Elly chooses the companies worth watching and hears when evidence changes enough to deserve attention, not whenever another article mentions their name.

## What it looks like

- Add a company to a watch with an explicit cadence, research allowance and the signals that matter.
- Receive a private summary of a meaningful new round, comparable sponsorship, relevant team change or contradiction.
- Open the before-and-after evidence, then choose whether to start a fresh event-matching run.
- Pause a watch and see its last actual check, next planned check and unresolved failures.

## Key details

- Builds on Sponsor Signal Timeline so every alert explains an evidenced change rather than repeating an old headline.
- Scheduled sponsor research is a future opt-in capability. The initial build remains operator-started.
- A watch is not evidence of buying intent and does not establish a live event match. Outreach still requires a fresh eligible event snapshot and the complete approval loop.
- Bound research work, deduplicate repeated news and honor exclusions. A missed or failed check is visible, not reported as no change.
- Notifications go only to the subscribing operator. No sponsor email, automatic follow-up or silent expansion of the maintained list.
