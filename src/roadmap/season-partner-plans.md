Some sponsors need a sustained community relationship, not another disconnected event pitch. Scout helps Elly build a thoughtful season proposal from opportunities that already have a defensible fit.

## What it looks like

- Select a season, audience or region and compare plausible sponsor plans across upcoming events.
- Astra explains the common thread, the strongest pairings and the needs that remain uncovered.
- Save alternative plans and choose which focused proposal to prepare first.
- Keep the original host, event dates, authoritative links and verbatim asks beside every proposed grouping.

## Key details

- Builds on the Event Coverage Map. Planning spans events; each outreach proposal still names at most three current matches and receives its own exact human approval.
- A plan is not a bundled price, exclusive sponsorship package or reservation. Each host controls its terms; no monetary commitment is inferred from an ask or funding announcement.
- Favor a coherent audience case over maximum email volume. Show weak overlaps rather than stretching the rationale to fill a season.
- Plans stay private until Elly deliberately shares an approved brief. Existing contacts and outreach are surfaced to avoid parallel or repetitive asks.
- Refresh eligibility before outreach. Changed dates or needs require a revised proposal and new review, while historical plans remain intact.

~~~
Planning does not relax qualification, match thresholds, per-message limits or duplicate-attempt protections. Use only authorized event facts; no CoHost write-back or invented sponsorship inventory is required.
~~~
