Let Astra help with the conversation without taking over Elly's inbox. Selected replies become concise context, suggested next steps and editable response proposals.

## What it looks like

- Paste or deliberately forward an individual reply into its sponsor conversation; unrelated inbox messages stay outside Scout.
- See the sender's actual words beside a suggested interpretation such as interested, wrong person or revisit later.
- Confirm the relationship update, correct the interpretation or ask for a response draft.
- Review the exact recipient and message before any reply leaves the app.

## Key details

- Builds on the Relationship Journal. Selective inbound capture and reply assistance are future additions; initial-build responses stay in Elly's inbox.
- A summary never becomes confirmed interest without Elly's review. Preserve message date, origin and relevant quoted evidence.
- An explicit opt-out blocks further outreach; an ambiguous stop request pauses dispatch for review. Suggestions never clear an existing exclusion or suppression.
- No full-mailbox ingestion, Gmail sign-in dependency, tracking pixels, automatic acknowledgments or autonomous follow-up sequence.
- Elly controls retained message content and deletion. Incoming text cannot authorize a send, alter permissions or expose another conversation.

~~~
Keep selective intake private and ownership-checked. Use Astra for proposed summaries and drafts; extend the existing exact-message approval and safe managed-sender rules to replies rather than creating a second, weaker send path.
~~~
