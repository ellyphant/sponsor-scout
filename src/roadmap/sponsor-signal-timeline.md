A company is not the same prospect forever. Turn saved assessments into a readable history of the evidence that strengthened, weakened or changed its sponsorship case.

## What it looks like

- Open a sponsor and compare two assessments side by side, including qualification factors, contact provenance and evidence gaps.
- A timeline calls out newly discovered funding announcements, comparable sponsorships, team changes and contradictory evidence.
- Each change links to both the previous case and the new source, with a plain explanation of its effect on qualification.

## Key details

- Run history and requalification are part of the approved initial-build scope. This future item adds evidence comparisons across those saved runs.
- Separate the date a fact was published from the date Scout first found it. Newly discovered does not necessarily mean newly happened.
- Missing evidence on a later run does not prove a team disappeared or a sponsor stopped participating.
- Keep original assessments, contacts and edited drafts unchanged. Changed event demand is distinct from changed sponsor evidence.
- This foundation compares completed research only. It does not schedule checks, send notifications or contact sponsors.
