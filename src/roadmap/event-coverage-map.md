Move from inspecting one event to understanding the whole set of opportunities. An event-first coverage view reveals where the desk has strong options, weak evidence or no useful path yet.

## What it looks like

- Compare upcoming events against distinct sponsor candidates across saved runs, with audience, city, date and the host's exact ask kept visible.
- Distinguish unresearched needs, strong evidence-backed matches, pending proposals and accepted outreach.
- Open any pairing to inspect its actual evidence and originating snapshot.
- Select neglected audience or regional clusters to guide the next deliberate scout run.

## Key details

- The approved initial Events screen includes snapshots and matched sponsors. This future item adds cross-run comparison and coverage planning, not basic event visibility.
- Coverage measures work and credible options, not money secured. No funding meter, invented budget gap or implied sponsorship commitment.
- Count distinct sponsor-event relationships, not repeated assessments or duplicate drafts.
- Match history stays dated. Changed or unavailable events cannot inherit an old fit score as though it were current.
- This view is private and read-only with respect to CoHost. Choosing a research priority neither changes a host's listing nor authorizes outreach.
