Replace a pile of changes with a short answer to: what deserves my attention this week? Scout connects meaningful sponsor developments to current event demand and explains the next useful decision.

## What it looks like

- An opt-in private brief highlights up to five opportunities, each with the sponsor change, relevant event, supporting sources and remaining gap.
- Recommendations distinguish research worth revisiting, a recipient that needs attention and an existing draft ready for a fresh review.
- Open the exact case from the brief instead of rebuilding context across runs.
- A quiet week says there are no strong opportunities rather than filling a quota.

## Key details

- Builds on watched evidence and adds prioritization across time, not the initial build's per-run queue or matching.
- Use a freshly checked event response. When current demand cannot be checked, report that limitation rather than recycle a historical match as current.
- Explain priority through audience fit, evidenced changes and actual dates. Do not label a company ready to buy or promise a response rate.
- Group related opportunities into one sponsor case and preserve the limit of three accepted events per outreach proposal. Surface existing outreach rather than multiplying drafts.
- The brief recommends actions; only Elly can approve an exact sponsor email. No sponsor messages are sent by opening or subscribing to it.
