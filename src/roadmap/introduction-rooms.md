Turn mutual interest into an introduction both sides understand. A small shared room carries the event, the actual ask and the agreed next step while Elly's research workspace stays private.

## What it looks like

- From a confirmed conversation, Elly selects an event and prepares a short introduction brief.
- Invite the sponsor and host separately after reviewing exactly what each person will see and receive.
- Once both consent, share the approved introduction and track acknowledgments, questions and the next agreed action.
- Close the room with a participant-confirmed or explicitly operator-recorded outcome.

## Key details

- Builds on reply context and confirmed relationship milestones. Guest participation is a deliberate future expansion beyond the single-operator console.
- Invitees see only their room, never the sponsor library, internal qualification scores, private notes or unrelated contact research.
- Confirm both parties' willingness before exchanging private contact details. Access can be revoked, but previously received email or copied material cannot be recalled.
- Every outbound invitation or sponsor/host message requires human approval. Opening a room does not start an automated sequence.
- Current event details remain subject to review. An introduction is not a reservation, signed sponsorship, guaranteed placement or payment.
- CoHost Club remains authoritative for its events and read-only to Scout. The room neither edits CoHost nor assumes a write endpoint exists.
